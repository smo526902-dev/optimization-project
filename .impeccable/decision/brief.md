## Scope

The Project 27 Streamlit app: four pages (Run, Explore, GA vs MA, Results). Mode: Operate. Shown on a laptop at arm's length; examiners may drive it alone.

## Task

Open on a live single run with defaults already computed. The examiner changes function, dimension or algorithm, runs again in seconds, watches the population converge, then races GA against MA over several seeds and reads an honest verdict. Proof is the team's own live implementation and the saved 30-run experiment; nothing is invented.

## Memorable moment

The population pinned on the landscape like specimens in a tray, the best one carrying a typed accession label that follows it as the generations play.

## Unresolved

None.
